Equipment and safety

Equipment

  • Bench power supply with current limit (6–12 V)
  • 5 V source for Test 2 (second supply channel, Arduino 5V pin or ESP32 VIN)
  • 2 × JGB37-520 12 V 530 rpm encoder motors, labelled M1, M2
  • 2 × BTS7960 driver boards, labelled D1, D2
  • ESP32 DevKit V1 (30 pin, headers soldered) and a USB data cable
  • Laptop with Arduino IDE 2, the esp32 by Espressif package (3.x) and the ESP32Encoder library (Kevin Harrington)
  • Breadboard, jumper wires, small screwdriver, multimeter, tape for marking the shaft
  • Sketch bts7960_test.ino (repo: SevvalNidaSagir/firmware/bts7960_test/)

Safety rules for every test

  1. Set the supply voltage and current limit before switching the output on.
  2. Never swap B+ and B−. The BTS7960 has no reverse-polarity protection.
  3. Never put 12 V on VCC. VCC takes only 3.3 V or 5 V.
  4. Encoder + (blue) goes to 3V3, never 5 V: ESP32 pins are not 5 V tolerant.
  5. All minus lines are joined: supply −, driver GND, ESP32 GND, encoder −.
  6. Before power-on, check with the multimeter: no beep between + and − of each rail.
  7. Switch the motor supply off before moving any wire.
  8. Keep the shaft free and hands clear: the motor starts as soon as power is on.
